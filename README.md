## What are Dots?

Based on the DevDay 2026 announcement and press coverage:

- **Always-on & proactive** — Dots run in the background and surface actions without being prompted, instead of waiting for you to open a chat.
- **Reachable anywhere** — text, call, Slack or email your Dot.
- **Own sandboxed VM** — each Dot runs in its own isolated computing environment and can work across apps and services.
- **Acts with approval** — can buy things for you after you approve.
- **Long-horizon tasks** — e.g. migrating an app off a deprecated API.
- **3D avatar** — every Dot gets a customizable avatar.
- **Availability** — rolling out to select users; Pro and Business Premium subscribers get one free Dot, with more available for purchase.

Features shown on stage alongside Dots: **Living Pages**, **Work with dots**, **Manage Pages**, **Presentations**.
